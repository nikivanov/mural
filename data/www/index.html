Drawable width
i
The drawable area is the rectangle Mural can physically reach between its two hanging points. It's 60% of the measured pin distance — the machine reserves a 20% margin on the left, right, and top edges so the pen can safely swing through hard turns. Height isn't limited by the machine, only by your paper.
